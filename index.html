About Don-Ann School

Determined to be the best.

Don-Ann School began in September 2015 as a small, family-founded initiative in Peace Estate, Lagos, built on a simple conviction: every child deserves an education that shapes both the mind and the character. Since then, we've grown, term by term, into a full learning community spanning Daycare through Senior Secondary (SSS 3) — still rooted in the same values we started with.

We are rooted in Christ and in good morals. At Don-Ann, discipline and diction are not afterthoughts; they are foundational, taught alongside every subject on the timetable. We believe a child who grows up grounded in strong values carries that foundation into every stage of life, long after they've left our classrooms — and it shows in our exam results.

What we offer

  • Daycare, Nursery, Primary and Secondary education — a curriculum that grows with your child from their earliest years through SSS 3.
  • Experienced, qualified teachers committed to bringing out the best in every learner.
  • A well-secured campus where parents can trust their children are safe throughout the school day.
  • 24/7 daycare service for families who need extended care.
  • A registered school nurse and sick bay on ground for immediate attention to any health need.
  • A functional library, science laboratory, ICT facility, and hands-on practical room — real, applied learning beyond the textbook.
  • A playground that supports healthy, active development alongside academics.

Why families choose us

Parents choose Don-Ann for three things above all: results, care, and character. We take academic performance seriously — our 2026 graduating class posted a 97% success rate in WAEC and a 90% success rate in JAMB. We take care seriously, from our sick bay to our secured campus. And we take moral formation seriously — because we believe a good education produces not just well-taught students, but well-raised young people.
